# 17 — Glossary and Traceability Index

## 1. Core Terms

**AI action:** An operation prepared or performed with AI involvement.

**Approval:** Explicit authorization for a defined action by an authorized Person.

**Authoritative object:** The product record that acts as the source of truth for an operational concept.

**Capture Item:** Preserved intake record before final classification.

**Case:** Multi-step coordinated outcome.

**Client:** Person receiving concierge service and holding authority over their personal information and approvals.

**Completion evidence:** Information supporting that the intended outcome occurred.

**Consequential action:** Action with external, financial, destructive, identity, privacy, medical, legal, ownership, or significant operational impact.

**Deterministic automation:** Approved rule-based operation whose behavior is constrained and explainable.

**Human verification:** Confirmation by an authorized Person that information or outcome is correct.

**Knowledge:** Reusable information with provenance, class, visibility, and trust level.

**Life Hub:** Secure domain organizing frequently required personal information.

**Notification:** Communication attempt intended to inform or prompt.

**Operator:** Person coordinating concierge operations.

**Personal Concierge Operating System:** Product integrating capture, work, information, time, finance, assets, communication, approvals, and human-controlled AI around a private concierge relationship.

**Provenance:** Origin and transformation history of information.

**Reminder:** Requirement to bring an underlying object to attention.

**Request Inbox:** Controlled review boundary between raw capture and authoritative objects.

**Source of truth:** Authoritative object or verified source for a fact or operational concept.

**Verified Fact:** Human-confirmed or trusted-source-confirmed Knowledge.

**Visibility:** Rule defining who may access an object or field and for what purpose.

---

## 2. Status Distinctions

**Draft:** Not yet authoritative or executable.

**Suggested:** AI or system proposal awaiting decision.

**Approved:** Authorized but not necessarily executed.

**Attempted:** Execution was initiated.

**Accepted externally:** External system acknowledged the request.

**Completed:** Workflow reports completion but may still require verification.

**Verified complete:** Responsible human confirmed the real-world outcome.

**Failed:** Intended operation did not complete.

**Partially completed:** Some but not all intended effects occurred.

## 4. Product Vision Traceability

This specification operationalizes the Product Vision principles:

- Capture becomes Universal Capture and Request Inbox.
- Understand and Organize become AI interpretation, confidence, provenance, and object proposals.
- Recommend becomes explainable AI Suggestions.
- Request Approval becomes the Approval object and consequence model.
- Execute Approved Actions becomes bounded, logged execution.
- Report Result becomes explicit result states.
- Human Verification becomes verified completion.
- Continuous Learning becomes controlled Knowledge and AI memory.

The Product Vision remains unchanged and authoritative. This suite supplies detailed operating rules.
