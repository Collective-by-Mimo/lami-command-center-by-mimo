# 13 — Long-Term Product Principles

## 1. Product Identity

LaMi is a Personal Concierge Operating System.

It exists to coordinate a person’s requests, information, relationships, obligations, assets, finances, communications, approvals, and time through a trusted operator and human-controlled AI assistance.

---

## 2. This Is Not a CRM

LaMi may contain People, Clients, Contacts, Providers, service history, preferences, and communications. These capabilities support personal concierge operations.

The product should not evolve into sales-pipeline management, lead scoring, mass outreach, marketing automation, or customer acquisition unless the product identity is explicitly changed.

---

## 3. This Is Not a Generic Project Manager

Tasks, Cases, milestones, assignments, and Calendars exist to fulfill personal concierge outcomes.

The product should avoid generic organizational complexity such as unlimited team hierarchies, arbitrary portfolio management, or broad workplace collaboration unless justified by the private concierge use case.

---

## 4. This Is Not an Accounting System

Finance capabilities coordinate obligations, spending, Bills, receipts, approvals, and reports.

The product must not claim formal accounting authority, tax correctness, banking capability, audited financial statements, or investment advice without separately approved scope and specialist requirements.

---

## 5. This Is Not Autonomous AI

AI prepares work, proposes structure, explains recommendations, performs approved deterministic operations, and executes bounded approved actions.

AI does not decide what is true, approve spending, infer consent, disclose sensitive information, or determine that consequential work is complete without appropriate human authority.

---

## 6. Durable Principles

- Human authority over automation.
- Capture before complexity.
- Organize before execution.
- Verify before completion.
- One authoritative object per operational concept.
- Every consequential action linked and traceable.
- Privacy by purpose.
- Conservative handling of sensitive information.
- Simplicity before platform expansion.
- Existing useful work before replacement.
- Explainable behavior before invisible intelligence.
- Recoverability before aggressive automation.

---

## 7. Product Quality Standard

A capability is mature when it is understandable, traceable, secure, recoverable, maintainable, proportionate in cost, and useful in daily operation—not merely when a screen or API exists.

---

## 8. Long-Term Success

The product succeeds when it reduces the mental burden of managing personal life while preserving dignity, privacy, ownership, and human judgment.

It should become more helpful through better context and workflows, not through greater autonomous authority.
